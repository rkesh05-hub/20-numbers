How to play

  1. Each turn draws a number from 100 to 999.
  2. Tap a lit slot to place it. The column must read low to high, 1 to 20.
  3. If the number has nowhere to go, the run ends. Fill every slot to win.

Win odds shows your chance of finishing from here if you play perfectly from now on. Tap the reel to skip the spin. Tap 4, 8, 12, 16 or 20 at the top to change the board size.
